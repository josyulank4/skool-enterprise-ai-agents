# Password Reset FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service portal and follow the emailed reset link. The link expires
after 30 minutes.

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include upper case, lower case, a
digit, and a symbol. Passwords expire every 90 days.

## Locked accounts
After five failed attempts your account locks for 30 minutes. Contact the
helpdesk if you need an immediate unlock.
