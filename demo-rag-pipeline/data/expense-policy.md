# Travel and Expense Policy

## Per-diem limits
Domestic travel: $75 per day for meals. International travel: $120 per day.
Lodging cap is $250 per night domestic and $350 per night international.

## Approval thresholds
Expenses under $500 require manager approval. Expenses from $500 to $5,000
require director approval. Anything above $5,000 requires VP approval.

## Receipts
Itemized receipts are required for every expense over $25. Missing receipts
must be documented with a signed affidavit within 10 business days.

## Reimbursement timeline
Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 14 calendar days of submission.
